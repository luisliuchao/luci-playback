About Luci Playback

Luci Playback is a free local player for Luci screen memory. It plays the frames and audio Luci saved on your computer. Choose your Luci folder in this browser. Files stay on this computer and are never uploaded.

Luci is the original screen-memory app from Memories.ai at luci.memories.ai. Luci Playback is an independent player for those local files. It is not the Luci app itself.

Which folder to pick

Pick the Luci home folder, not your user folder. Chrome will refuse a folder that contains system files.

  • macOS: ~/.luciMicrosoft
  • Linux or Windows: ~/.luci

On a Mac, click Choose folder, press Cmd+Shift+G, paste ~/.luciMicrosoft, and open it.

Privacy

Frames, audio, and the Safe Storage password stay on this computer. The browser decrypts LUCISS01 frames locally with WebCrypto. The password never leaves this computer. This browser keeps the unlock so you can refresh.

Playback

Playback follows real capture time. The scrub bar is the captured day, not frame count. The clock shows the time of the current frame. If the Luci folder has microphone or system audio, it plays in sync.

Questions

Does this upload my screen memory?
No. Nothing is uploaded. The site only asks the browser to read a folder you pick.
Do I need an account?
No. Open the player and choose a folder.
How do I unlock encrypted frames on a Mac?
Copy the command from the unlock screen and run it in Terminal. That puts the Luci Safe Storage password on your clipboard. Paste it into the field.
