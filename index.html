Luci Playback

Luci Playback is a local player for Luci screen memory. It plays the frames and audio Luci saved on your computer. Choose your Luci folder in this browser. Files stay on this computer and are never uploaded.

Luci is the original screen-memory app from Memories.ai at luci.memories.ai.

Pick ~/.luciMicrosoft on a Mac, or ~/.luci on Linux or Windows. Do not pick the user folder.
